About SkillGap AI

SkillGap AI is an AI-powered platform that helps students and job seekers analyze their resumes, identify missing skills, and receive personalized career recommendations based on industry requirements.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry needs, ensuring everyone is placement-ready for their dream roles.
